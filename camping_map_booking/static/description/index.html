Camping Map Booking

Turn your campsite plan into a clickable map where guests pick a pitch by tapping it - free, occupied and unsuitable spots colored live from your actual bookings.

Odoo 19.0 Website + Backend Works with Planning & Renting

What it does

Upload any site photo or plan, draw clickable zones over it, and link each zone to the pitches it represents. That's it - the map handles selection, availability and vehicle suitability on its own.

1

Draw zones on your own image

Outline each pitch, cabin or bungalow as a polygon over your campsite photo or plan - no fixed grid, no generic icons.

2

Live availability coloring

Zones are colored free, occupied or not suitable in real time, computed from existing planning.slot bookings for the dates being checked.

3

Vehicle-aware suitability

Restrict a pitch to certain vehicle types (tent, car, caravan, motorhome, camper van) via its planning role - unsuitable pitches are flagged before a guest picks one.

4

One map, two places

The exact same component shows up during pitch selection at website checkout and in the backend preview - what staff see while calibrating is what guests see live.

5

Generate zones from resources

Already have pitches set up as resources? One click creates a starter zone for each one, ready to be outlined.

6

Built-in testing tool

Simulate any stay and vehicle type from the backend "Testing" tab to confirm colors and calibration before publishing.

Availability, at a glance

Every zone's color comes from a single configurable source of truth, so the legend on the map always matches what's actually bookable.

Setting it up

From a blank map to a bookable one, in a few steps.

  1. Create a campsite map and upload its image. Go to Camping Maps and open (or create) a record with your site photo or plan.
  2. Generate or draw zones. Use "Create Zone for Each Resource" for existing pitches, or add zones by hand and outline each on the image.
  3. Calibrate against the real image. Compare the overlay to the photo on the "Map Preview" tab and adjust polygon points until they line up.
  4. Test with real dates and a vehicle. Use the "Testing" tab to preview free/occupied/not-suitable colors for a chosen stay before going live.
  5. Publish. Once pitches are rentable products, the same map appears automatically during pitch selection at checkout.
